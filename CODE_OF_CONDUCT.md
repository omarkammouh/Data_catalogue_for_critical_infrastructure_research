# Code of conduct

Contributors and users should treat one another respectfully. Discuss evidence and proposed changes without personal attacks. Welcome questions and contributions from people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Harassment, discriminatory language, intimidation and publication of private information without consent are not acceptable. Keep discussion relevant to the catalogue and its use.

The maintainer may edit or remove inappropriate content, close discussions or restrict participation. Decisions should be proportionate and explained where doing so does not expose private information. Report concerns privately to the maintainer using the professional contact listed on [Omar Kammouh's TU Delft profile](https://www.tudelft.nl/en/staff/o.kammouh/). Do not post sensitive details in a public issue.
