# Worked research examples

These examples illustrate discovery using resources in version 1.0.0. They do not establish that the resources are interchangeable or suitable for every study.

## Find inputs for a water-network study

Select **Data** and the **Drinking water** sector, then search for `hydraulic`. Open [DiTEC-WDN](https://omarkammouh.github.io/Data_catalogue_for_critical_infrastructure_research/?r=data-ditec-wdn-hydraulic-scenarios), which describes simulated hydraulic scenarios across water distribution networks. Inspect the normal-operation scope and the distinction between simulated states and field observations.

Follow the provider's access link and check formats, dataset size and licence before obtaining it. Compare its network and variable definitions with the requirements of [EPANET](https://omarkammouh.github.io/Data_catalogue_for_critical_infrastructure_research/?r=model-epanet). The two records help locate relevant materials; they do not establish that every data file can be used directly in every EPANET version.

## Compare electricity models

Select **Model**, **Electricity**, and a use such as **Optimisation**. Open [pandapower](https://omarkammouh.github.io/Data_catalogue_for_critical_infrastructure_research/?r=model-pandapower) and [PyPSA-Earth](https://omarkammouh.github.io/Data_catalogue_for_critical_infrastructure_research/?r=model-pypsa-earth).

The descriptions distinguish network analysis and power-flow functions from a geographically extended energy-system workflow with capacity-expansion uses. Compare spatial scope, required inputs, solver dependencies and licence terms. Export a shortlist with the selected filters to record the alternatives considered. Consult the original model documentation before selecting a method.

## Find applications of transport-disruption analysis

Select **Case study** and **Public transit**, then search for `London`. The record [Impacts of four London Tube strikes on bikeshare use and cycling behaviour](https://omarkammouh.github.io/Data_catalogue_for_critical_infrastructure_research/?r=case_study-london-tube-strikes-bikeshare-disruption) describes a study of mode substitution and station capacity during disruptions.

Read the study scope and its caveats, then follow the article DOI. Use its resource references as leads for data and modelling choices. Similar events elsewhere may require different demand assumptions and local observations. Share the filtered catalogue URL with collaborators and cite the original study in subsequent research.
